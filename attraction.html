Top Attractions

Shenzhen blends fast-moving modern ambition with pockets of nature, novelty, and seaside calm. Three places that capture the city's range, all within the vast subway system. A theme park that puts the world in walking distance to parks where the skyline disappears behind the trees.

Window of the World

A theme park that lets you walk past the Eiffel Tower, Pyramids of Giza, Taj Mahal, and dozens of other world wonders in a single afternoon. Built at scaled-down sizes, it's one of the most popular ways to see "the world" without leaving Shenzhen.

Bay Park (Shenzhen Bay)

China's version of San Francisco Bay, with the offices of the country's biggest tech giants from Alibaba to Tencent. A location that offers some of the best skyline views in the city. Come here at sunset for the full effect with the city lights coming on.

Lianhuashan Park

A green hill in the middle of the city where forest trails lead to one of the best skyline views in Shenzhen. Locals come here to fly kites, walk their dogs, and enjoy the city's greenery. It's a perfect place to take a break from the city life and enjoy the natural greenery.

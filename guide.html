Food Guide

Shenzhen's food scene runs the full range, from popular milk tea chains, fresh seafood off the dock, modern Cantonese fine dining, and cheap dim sum restaurants.

Chagee

Locations across Shenzhen — flagship at MixC World, Luohu District

The country's favorite premium milk tea brand. Chagee focuses on using real high quality fresh-brewed tea, and their signature Bo Ya Jue Xian jasmine milk tea has become the order to beat in mainland China.

Visit Website

Hai DaDa ZhengQi Seafood

2F Siji Lijing Building, No. 34 Jinji Road, Nanshan District, Shenzhen China

Pick your seafood live from the tanks and have it steamed to order. King crab, lobster, geoduck, and abalone are the popular dishes. Simple dishes with rich flavors that let the freshness do the work.

Visit Website

Xin Rong Ji

L6, Ping An Finance Center North Tower, Yitian Road

A Michelin-starred Cantonese fine-dining destination known for its yellow croaker, dry-aged duck, and a wine cellar that takes the meal an extra step beyond. Reservations are a must on weekends.

Visit Website

Shang Palace

Level 2 of Hotel, East Side, Railway Station, 1002 Jianshe Road, Shenzhen

One of Shenzhen's most popular dim sum destinations. Expect incredible har gow, char siu bao, and a quiet, classic dining room. Go for weekend lunch or early breakfast when the carts are at their best.

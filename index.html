About Shenzhen

Where the future meets the bay

Shenzhen is China's youngest and fastest growing city. It was built from a fishing village in 40 years into a landscape of skyscrapers, parks, and high valued tech companies concentrated in South China.

Drones flying across the city, EVs in every lane, dim sum restaurants in every street. If you want a mix of San Francisco and Hong Kong, this is the place to go.
